FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Geovani Construction holds California contractor licence CSLB #1087004 and is fully bonded and insured.

Do you offer financing?

We work with reputable unsecured home improvement financing partners. Many qualified homeowners can get approved without using home equity. Approval and terms are set by the lender.

What areas do you serve?

Palm Springs and the surrounding Coachella Valley communities. Call us if you are unsure whether you are in range.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. Room remodels typically run 4–8 weeks; additions and exteriors take longer. You get a timeline with your estimate.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Site visits and written estimates are free across our service area.

Do you handle permits?

For work that requires them, yes — we manage the permit process as part of the job.

Our story

About Geovani Construction

Geovani began his career in construction more than 20 years ago as a helper. Over the following two decades he worked on hundreds of projects across Southern California, steadily building skill, knowledge, and a strong reputation for reliable workmanship.

Four years ago he successfully passed the California General Contractor licence exam and established Geovani Construction as a fully licensed contractor (CSLB #1087004).

Today the company is family-supported and driven by a genuine belief in the American dream. Geovani treats every home as if it were his own and stands behind the quality of the work.

When you choose Geovani Construction, you are choosing experience, integrity, and a contractor who takes pride in every job.
